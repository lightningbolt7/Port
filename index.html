About

I'm a Computer Science student at The University of Texas at Dallas, originally from India. I'm drawn to how technology works under the hood — and to solving problems efficiently. (Being a little lazy turns out to be great motivation for writing good automation.)

I started with Java in high school, picked up Python and SQL, and taught myself web development after my first hackathon showed me what I didn't yet know. Since then I've built full-stack apps, a computer-vision mobile app, a Discord bot for an 800-member community, and AI/ML projects that have earned three hackathon awards — including first place at HackUTD.

Right now I'm most interested in applied machine learning and building products where a model is only one part of a well-engineered system — and, since 2026, in making games with Unity.
