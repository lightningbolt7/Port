    Arcform

    File conversion, one right-click away.

    Arcform brings file conversion into the Windows right-click menu. Instead of uploading files to online converters, you right-click and choose what you need.

    • Convert Word ↔ PDF, iPhone HEIC photos, PNG / JPG / WEBP, and MOV / MKV / WEBM / AVI videos to MP4 or MP3.
    • Shrink to fit images and videos under 1, 10 or 25 MB, with a quality search for images and size-targeted two-pass encoding for video.
    • Copy as converts a file straight to the clipboard, ready to paste into chats and emails.
    • Merge PDFs from any folders with a merge queue and a drag-to-reorder editor.
    How it stays fast

    Selecting many files opens a single progress window: extra processes pass their files to the first one over a named pipe. Jobs run in parallel lanes matched to each tool's limits. Arcform also avoids slow work where it can, by keeping one copy of Word open for a whole batch and copying H.264 video into MP4 without re-encoding.
